# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Nunchuck
  - Two 50 cm hardwood handles on a 40 cm steel chain
  - Hold one handle; the other swings on the chain with real physics
  - Pick it up by either end
  - Damage from the free handle's speed on a logarithmic curve: 80 at 8 m/s up to 200 at 30 m/s; slower hits don't count
  - Anything within 10 cm of the free handle as it swings past counts as hit
  - Only hits that count use charge
  - Fast hits push enemies back, and hits of 16 m/s or more stun them for 1.5 s
  - Hurts other players it hits, never the one holding it
  - Same price as the baseball bat
- Scythe
  - A long, crooked scythe held by the end of its shaft, reaching towards your crosshair; the blade sticks out to one side; press E to flip it to the other side
  - Scrolling it out is smooth; scrolling it back in tugs it towards you with a sword swing; cuts that land play a sword slice
  - Only pulling cuts: hook an enemy and pull back (or back away)
  - 150 damage per cut (a pull of at least 2 m/s); 12 uses per charge
  - Cuts drag the enemy towards you; friendly fire
  - Same price as the baseball bat
