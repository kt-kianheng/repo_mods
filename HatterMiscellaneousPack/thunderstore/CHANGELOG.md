# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Added the Pocket Enemy Compass and the Pocket Extraction Compass (consumables, $5K and $2K): break one with [E] for an on-screen 3D arrow to the nearest enemy / extraction point, for 5 minutes.
- Added the All-Seeing Monocle: look through it with [E] to see valuables within 10 m through walls (handles like a drone); 10 charges of 10 s per battery.
